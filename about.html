Why Orchestral

Orchestral exists because great companies rarely fail from a lack of ambition. They struggle when the human side of the business does not evolve as fast as the business itself. Growth outpaces process, managers are promoted faster than they are developed, and the systems that once worked for 15 employees quietly stop working at 80.

Orchestral closes that gap.

Our Promise: We build the people systems, leadership capability, and cultural foundation that give organizations a genuine competitive advantage as they grow, without losing what makes them great in the first place: their people.

"Optimizing the people foundation that allows your business to perform at its best."
02

What Sets Orchestral Apart

  • Embedded, not transactional. Orchestral operates as an extension of the leadership team, not a vendor that shows up for a project and disappears. Context and trust compound over time instead of resetting with every engagement.
  • Executive-level judgment at every stage. Clients get direct access to senior HR leadership, as complex decisions require executive-level thinking, not generic advice.
  • A full team behind every engagement. Orchestral's bench includes specialists in performance management, compensation, talent, and sector-specific expertise, deployed exactly when the work calls for it.
  • Built on diagnosis, not assumptions. Every partnership starts by understanding the current state before recommending a solution, so what gets built actually fits the business.
  • Flexible by design. Support flexes with the business: heavier hours when active project work is underway, lighter once a workstream reaches steady-state advisory.
  • Pragmatic and practical HR advice vs. red tape. Compliance is the floor, not the ceiling. HR governance is handled as a foundational layer under everything Orchestral does to mitigate people risk.

The Founder

"Every business is only as strong as its people strategy." Valerie Khan

Valerie Khan

Founder, Orchestral HR Consulting

Valerie brings over 20 years of executive HR leadership to every client engagement, having built and led people strategy for organizations ranging from Fortune 500 institutions to fast-growing insurance and financial services firms. Most recently Vice President of Human Resources at a national insurance brokerage network, Valerie partnered directly with company presidents to drive organizational effectiveness, talent strategy, and culture transformation across a multi-brand portfolio, successfully integrating multiple M&A transactions and driving performance while preserving the entrepreneurial spirit of each business.

Her career spans Navacord, CIBC, RSA, GE, BMO, CPP Investment Board, and the Toronto Police Service, giving her a rare depth of experience across regulated industries, complex union environments, manufacturing operations, and high-growth corporate settings.

Valerie works alongside leadership teams as an extension of the business, delivering both strategic advisory and practical, on-the-ground execution so owners can focus on running their companies while Orchestral handles the people side.
