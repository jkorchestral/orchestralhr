How We Work

Every workstream Orchestral takes on follows the same three-step approach, regardless of scope. Nothing gets rebuilt or recommended until we understand how your business actually operates.

Diagnose
A diagnostic phase reviews current state: existing documents, processes, and leadership discussion to gain a deeper understanding of how things actually work in practice across the business, not just on paper.
Recommend
A recommendation phase delivers what you can act on to make the most impact, such as rebuilt templates, a standardized process, defined criteria, or a new framework, scoped to fit your actual structure rather than a generic template.
Implement
The implementation phase involves partnering directly with leaders to embed people practices that not only work but stick.
