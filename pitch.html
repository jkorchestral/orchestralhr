Proactive People Practices vs. Reactive

People issues are cost effective to resolve while they're still a single note out of place, and before they turn into full discord. Here's what waiting on this costs, and how staying in tune protects your bottom line.

♫

Fine-Tuning the Numbers

A leader spending 20% of their time (up to a full day a week) off-script on people issues instead of focusing on driving revenue and production, that's roughly $40,000 a year of leadership capacity spent troubleshooting instead of conducting the business forward.

♪

The Business Case: 100 Employees, 15% Turnover

Here's the costs and what changes when turnover drops by 5 pts. to 10% with issues getting caught early instead of managed in crisis.

At 15% turnover, that's ~$460,200 a year

15 replacements × $30,680 average cost per turnover event. Drop turnover 5 points to 10%, and you save ~$153,400/year.*
